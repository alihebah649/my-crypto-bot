# Halal Spot Trading Universe

## Current expansion

The Paper Trading universe was expanded from 16 to 22 Binance Spot USDT pairs.

Added:

- XRPUSDT — XRP
- XLMUSDT — Stellar
- HBARUSDT — Hedera
- SUIUSDT — Sui
- BCHUSDT — Bitcoin Cash
- TRXUSDT — TRON

The additions were selected using two practical gates:

1. A current external Shariah screen classified the underlying crypto asset as Halal.
2. The asset has an established Binance Spot USDT market and sufficient market activity to be considered for this bot's existing short-term strategy.

## Important Shariah qualification

There is no universally binding AAOIFI crypto standard, and contemporary scholars differ on the permissibility of cryptocurrency itself. The project therefore treats the screen as a documented research filter, not as a personal fatwa. The bot uses Spot ownership only and does not use leverage, futures, margin, lending, or interest-bearing earn products.

The external screen used for this expansion currently lists XRP, XLM, HBAR, SUI, BCH and TRX as Halal with 85/100 (A) under its published AAOIFI-aligned methodology. See the dated research sources used for this decision before any live deployment.

## Explicit exclusions

- TONUSDT was not added because Binance replaced TON spot pairs with GRAM spot pairs in July 2026.
- Meme/gambling/prediction tokens were not considered.
- Stablecoins and yield-bearing assets were not added because their reserve/yield structures require separate Shariah review.
- DeFi governance/lending tokens were not added merely because they are liquid; their underlying financial mechanisms require a separate review.

## Re-screening rule

This list must be re-screened before Live Trading and after material protocol/tokenomics changes. A Halal label from a third-party screener is evidence for research, not a guarantee of religious permissibility.
